# Scholarly Corrections and Qualification Changes

## Psalm 17 source-access and qualification correction

- **Evidence ID:** `psalm-17-textual-proposals-verses-11-and-14`
- **Anchor:** Psalm 17:11, 14.
- **Previous wording:** the evidence and its source record said the English
  abstract had been consulted, the Italian article had not been reviewed, and
  its full comparison of Hebrew witnesses and ancient versions was unavailable.
- **Verified problem:** ED-3 independently read the official full Italian
  article, especially pp. 398–402 and 406–407. It confirms that v. 11 is a
  conjectural proposal and v. 14 prefers the Masoretic *qere*. The article
  records no consensus and warns that the BHK/BHS report of manuscript support
  for the v. 11 conjecture is probably unfounded. ED-3 did not inspect those
  critical editions or manuscript images.
- **Corrected wording:** source metadata now records the full-text inspection
  and page range. The interpretation retains both as attributed editorial
  judgments, adds the article's no-consensus and apparatus caution, and says
  the underlying apparatus was not independently checked. See the CKL record
  and source entry in `psalms.json`.
- **Supporting source and locator:** Stanisław Bazyliński, “Annotazioni sul
  testo del Salmo 17,” *The Biblical Annals* 11(3) (2021), 387–410,
  especially 398–402 and 406–407; [official journal record and PDF](https://czasopisma.kul.pl/index.php/ba/article/view/12435).
- **Confidence/dispute:** confidence remains low; textual variant remains
  disputed; no claim about the original reading was strengthened.
- **Reader implication:** readers can see that an article-level argument was
  read, while manuscript support and original priority remain unresolved.
- **Human review:** required. A Hebrew-text specialist should inspect the
  critical editions and the article's apparatus claims before publication.
