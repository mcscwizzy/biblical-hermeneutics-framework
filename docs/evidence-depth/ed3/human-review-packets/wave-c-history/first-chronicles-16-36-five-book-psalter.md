## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

First Chronicles 16:36 ends with the gathered people saying “Amen” and praising YHWH; its wording closely matches Psalm 106:48. The surrounding hymn also draws on material found in Psalms 105, 96, and 106.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Longacre argues that 1 Chronicles 16:36 preserves Psalm 106:48 in its role as the doxology closing book four of the Psalter. He reads this reuse, together with other Psalm material in the hymn, as evidence that the Chronicler knew a five-book collection. He acknowledges proposals that the doxology originally belonged to Psalm 106 itself and leaves open the possibility that individual psalms entered the collection later; the exact date of Chronicles is also uncertain.
