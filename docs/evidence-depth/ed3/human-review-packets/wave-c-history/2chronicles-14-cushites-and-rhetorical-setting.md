## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Second Chronicles 14:9-15 names Zerah the Cushite and describes a very large army with chariots; the episode is not narrated in the Kings account of Asa's reign, while 2 Chronicles 16:8 later recalls Cushites and Libyans.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Jonker argues that the army figures are metaphorical rather than exact indications of troop strength. Because the Kings Vorlage has no account of this same battle, he proposes that the Chronicler included the Cushite enemy for rhetorical purposes and places this within his proposed Persian-period setting for Chronicles. The textual difference does not independently determine whether a conflict occurred, and the composition date remains a scholarly reconstruction.
