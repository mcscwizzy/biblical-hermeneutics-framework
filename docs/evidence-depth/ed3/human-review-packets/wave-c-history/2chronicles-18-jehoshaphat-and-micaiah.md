## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The Micaiah and Ramoth-gilead narrative occurs in both 1 Kings 22:1-40 and 2 Chronicles 18:1-34; Chronicles continues with Jehoshaphat's rebuke in 19:1-3.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Dillard observes that the Kings context emphasizes the death of Ahab, while the Chronicler makes the incident a significant problem in Jehoshaphat's reign and interprets it through the rebuke that follows. He also argues that the Chronicler patterns portions of Jehoshaphat's story after Asa's. These are literary-theological readings of the parallel narratives, not separate external attestations.
