## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The KCI abstract identifies 2 Kings 14:1-20 as the parallel source for the 2 Chronicles 25:1-28 account and reports that the Chronicler retained some material, altered or deleted portions, and added unique material.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Hwang interprets these changes as emphasizing causality and retributive justice, connecting the immediate and wider Chronicles context, highlighting Amaziah's sin, and defending Judah's legitimacy in relation to the Davidic covenant. These are the abstract's reported conclusions; the article's detailed argument and alternatives remain to be reviewed.
