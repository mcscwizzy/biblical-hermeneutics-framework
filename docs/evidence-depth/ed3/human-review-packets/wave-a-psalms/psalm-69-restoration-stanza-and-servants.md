## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 69:34–36 in BHF numbering calls the heavens, earth, and seas to praise, then describes Zion’s rescue and rebuilding, the inheritance of the servants’ offspring, and life in the city. Lyons numbers this closing unit 69:35–37 because his discussion counts the superscription as verse 1.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Lyons argues that the change from an individual complaint to cosmic praise and restored Zion, together with shared language about the servants’ offspring and inheritance in Isaiah 65:9, supports reading the closing unit as a later addition that coordinates the psalm with Isaiah 40–66. The proposal concerns literary shaping; it does not establish a direct scribal borrowing route or settle whether an earlier psalmic core existed.
