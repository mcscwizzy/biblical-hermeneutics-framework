## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 143:2 confesses that no living person is righteous before God. The Greek wording is taken up in Galatians 2:16c; the question addressed by Moore and Bachmann is whether it also supplies a premise in Paul’s argument in Galatians 3:10.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Moore argues that the confession of universal human sinfulness in Psalm 143:2 informs Paul’s denial of justification by works and his conclusion in Galatians 3:10. Bachmann accepts the wording’s echo in Galatians 2:16c but rejects the premise Moore assigns it in 3:10, arguing that Galatians 3:10–12 uses two syllogisms with premises from Deuteronomy 27:26, Habakkuk 2:4, and Leviticus 18:5. This record preserves the disagreement as later reception and does not create a curated relationship route.
