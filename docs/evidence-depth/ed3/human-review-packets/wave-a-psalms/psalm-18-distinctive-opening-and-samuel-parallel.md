## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The Psalm’s opening line uses a qal form of the Hebrew root rhm, commonly rendered “I love you,” and Lee reports that the line does not appear in the parallel song in 2 Samuel 22.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Lee argues that the Psalmic form revises and clarifies the Samuel version and treats the unusual rhm form in light of Aramaic usage and Psalm 116. This is his linguistic/editorial reconstruction; the accessible English summary does not permit independent review of the Korean analysis.
