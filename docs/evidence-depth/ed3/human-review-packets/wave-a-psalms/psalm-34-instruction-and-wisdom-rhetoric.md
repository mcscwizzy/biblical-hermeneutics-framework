## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 34:11-14 (BHF numbering) addresses hearers as children, invites them to listen, and teaches the fear of YHWH through instructions about speech, peace, and life.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Brown compares Psalm 34’s address and instruction with Proverbs and argues that shared wisdom rhetoric can be studied without classifying the whole poem as a “wisdom psalm.” The chapter’s accessible excerpt explicitly treats that genre label as methodologically unsettled.
