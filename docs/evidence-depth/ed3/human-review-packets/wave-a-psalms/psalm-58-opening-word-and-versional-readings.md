## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Begg’s abstract identifies competing interpretations of the opening word: the Masoretic vocalization is commonly read with a sense of silence or muteness, while ancient-version renderings reflect other understandings. The article numbers the opening colon Psalm 58:2a, counting the superscription as verse 1; this is BHF Psalm 58:1a.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Begg surveys earlier proposals and offers a proposal of his own, but the accessible abstract does not provide enough of its argument to state or assess that proposal. This record therefore preserves the existence and nature of the versional disagreement without asserting an original reading.
