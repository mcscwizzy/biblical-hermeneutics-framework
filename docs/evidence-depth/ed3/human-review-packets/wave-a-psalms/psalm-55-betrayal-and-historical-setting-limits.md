## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The speaker describes city violence and strife (vv. 9–11), then names the betrayer as an equal, companion, and familiar friend (vv. 12–14). No city, companion, ruler, or occasion is named.

**Scholarly interpretation**

DeClaissé-Walford calls the historical occasion virtually impossible to recover. She surveys exile and David/Ahithophel proposals and reports Bail’s feminist reading as an interpretive possibility, not a historical identification.
