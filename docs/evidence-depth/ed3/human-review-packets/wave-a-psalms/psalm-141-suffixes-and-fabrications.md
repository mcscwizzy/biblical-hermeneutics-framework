## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Booij’s abstract discusses source verses 4–6, where he assigns the third-person plural suffixes to the “workers of mischief”; it also proposes “fabrications” for ʿalilot and a reading of ʿod as “in the end.” His numbering counts the superscription as verse 1, so the cited unit maps to BHF Psalm 141:3–5.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Booij argues that these grammatical and lexical choices support a reading in which the petitioner resists the wrongdoers’ intrigues and seeks discipline. The broader national-distress setting appears in the abstract but is not adopted here because the full argument was not accessible.
