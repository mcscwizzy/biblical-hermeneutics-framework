## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 21:2 says that the king’s request was not withheld. Sheveka’s abstract identifies the Hebrew noun for “request” as a hapax legomenon and compares the phrase with diplomatic correspondence in Amarna Letter EA 9.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Sheveka proposes that the expression evokes the formal “brotherhood” relation between equal kings and that court scribes adapted diplomatic language for a king’s address to God. This is a specific lexical-historical proposal, not proof that the psalm was composed in a royal chancery or that it directly borrowed EA 9. The article’s full text and underlying letter discussion were not accessible.
