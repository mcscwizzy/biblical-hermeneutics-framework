## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 91 combines images of refuge and shelter with a way protected from stumbling, fear of night terror, and a concluding divine speech promising rescue to the one who calls.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Botha compares the psalm's language of walking safely, protection of the foot, and freedom from fear with Proverbs 3. He reads Psalm 91's current form as wisdom-oriented instruction and argues that it also influenced Job 5:17–26. This item records his literary proposal without treating dependence direction, a temple origin, or a date as settled.
