## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The poem begins with a cry from distress, turns to sustained questions and recollection, and closes by recounting God's way through the waters and the leading of the people.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Kamuwanga describes a lament moving from complaint to confidence and praise and observes that it has no explicit petition. He reads recollection of divine action as a reorientation of the sufferer. His proposed exilic setting and broader chaos imagery are not established by the poem alone and are not treated here as composition history.
