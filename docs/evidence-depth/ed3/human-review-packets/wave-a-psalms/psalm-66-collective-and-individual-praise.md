## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Plural speech and communal recollection dominate vv. 1–12; at v. 13 the speaker shifts to singular vows, offerings, and testimony through v. 20.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Sutton analyzes vv. 1b–12 as collective praise and vv. 13–20 as individual praise, linking both to Exodus memory and worship imagery. His post-exilic framing concerns final Psalter shaping, not the date of original composition; his pandemic application is modern reception.
