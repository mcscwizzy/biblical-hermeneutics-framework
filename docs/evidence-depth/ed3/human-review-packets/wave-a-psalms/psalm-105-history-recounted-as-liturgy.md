## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 105 addresses a worshipping audience and recounts divine action from ancestral promises through the exodus and settlement traditions as grounds for praise and remembrance.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Gillingham's abstract proposes that Psalms 104–106 form a liturgical collection and that historical recollection in Psalms 105–106 enabled participation in Second Temple worship and teaching through song. Because the full article was not accessible, this item attributes only that abstract-level proposal and does not treat the psalm as an independent historical source for each event it narrates.
