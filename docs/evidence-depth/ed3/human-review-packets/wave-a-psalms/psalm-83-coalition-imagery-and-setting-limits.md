## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The poem names multiple neighboring peoples as a coalition against Israel and includes Assyria in the list. It does not name a date, ruler, battle, or treaty.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Mitchell notes that some names may recall earlier conflict traditions but argues that the ten-nation list, especially its inclusion of Assyria, is difficult to reduce to one local alliance from Jehoshaphat’s time. In his canonical reading, the coalition is archetypal and eschatological. This is Mitchell’s interpretation in a PhD thesis; the poem alone does not establish that date, a specific historical coalition, or a precise eschatological timetable. No cross-passage relationship is authored.
