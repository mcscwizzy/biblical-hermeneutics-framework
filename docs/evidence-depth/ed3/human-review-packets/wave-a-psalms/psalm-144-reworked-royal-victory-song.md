## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 144 combines a royal speaker trained for battle, a meditation on human frailty, and a request for rescue. Zenger identifies it as the final royal psalm before Psalm 145 and interprets its relation to Psalm 18 as a shift from victory thanksgiving to supplication.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Zenger argues that Psalm 144 rereads Psalm 18: the king wins only because YHWH rescues him. He also sees the poem’s anthropology as lowering the king from divine status while leaving him the object of divine care and service. These are attributed final-form proposals; the psalm does not identify a specific historical battle.
