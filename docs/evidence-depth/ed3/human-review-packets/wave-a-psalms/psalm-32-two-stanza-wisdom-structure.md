## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 32 repeats forgiveness and sin language in vv. 1-5 and links vv. 6-11 through repetitions about protection, joy, and the righteous; v. 8 shifts into direct instruction.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Potgieter proposes a unified wisdom composition in two stanzas, 1-5 and 6-11, each with three corresponding strophes. He reads confession and acceptance of YHWH’s guidance as one pedagogical movement. He notes alternatives that posit a thanksgiving core with later wisdom framing, so the compositional conclusion is disputed.
