## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The poem says YHWH loves Zion’s gates more than Jacob’s other dwellings, names Rahab, Babylon, Philistia, Tyre, and Cush, and repeats the statement that a person was born there.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Rautenbach reads Psalm 87 in its Korahite context and through proposed ancient Near Eastern patronage, group identity, and honour values. She argues that the foreign names accent Zion’s distinct status rather than granting all peoples equal citizenship there. Other interpreters cited by her understand Zion as home to or mother of the nations. The short poem’s structure and the force of its repeated birth formula remain disputed, so neither reading is treated as settled.
