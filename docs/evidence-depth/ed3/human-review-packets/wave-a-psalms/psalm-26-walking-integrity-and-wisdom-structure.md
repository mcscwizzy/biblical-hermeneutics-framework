## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The speaker opens and closes by describing a walk in integrity, asks YHWH to test heart and kidneys, distinguishes from violent company, and declares love for YHWH’s house and altar. The poem is not an alphabetic acrostic.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Potgieter argues that the repeated walking motif, concentric structure, and language shared with Israelite wisdom traditions make sapiential influence visible in a poem often classified as cultic. Her proposal offers a way to read ethical conduct and temple imagery together. The present text does not establish a precise performance setting or the Persian-period date proposed in the article.
