## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 29 depicts YHWH’s voice over waters and in thunder and names Lebanon, Sirion, and wilderness in its storm poem; this imagery has prompted Ugaritic comparisons.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Töyräänvuori proposes triangulating Psalm 29 with the Baal Cycle, the separate Baal enthronement hymn KTU 1.101, and Syrian glyptic depictions. The abstract supports the method only; it does not establish direct literary dependence, an original Baal hymn, or a composition date.
