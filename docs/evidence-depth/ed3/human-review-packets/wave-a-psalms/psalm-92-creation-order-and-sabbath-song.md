## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

In the Masoretic poem, a short confession about YHWH's exaltation stands at its center, framed by praise for steadfast love, creation, and the flourishing of the righteous.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Tucker counts seven couplets/triplets on either side of the central monocolon and argues that this placement reinforces the confession that YHWH orders the world. He links the poem's creation imagery and Sabbath superscription to that claim, but cautions against forced chiastic readings. His exile-oriented setting is an interpretation, not a secure date.
