## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The superscription associates Psalm 56 with David and his encounter in Gath; the prayer names fear, trust in God, hostile speech, and a promise of praise.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Potgieter treats the superscription as a literary/metatextual cue that invites readers to connect the speaker's fear with David's fear in 1 Samuel 21 and to read the prayer as a model of trust. This is an interpretation of the Psalter's heading, not independent evidence that the event occurred as described or that David composed the poem.
