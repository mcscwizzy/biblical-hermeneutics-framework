## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The article identifies textual and translation difficulties at its verses 11 and 14. It proposes a conjectural reading at v. 11, “they have advanced against me; now they have encircled me,” and prefers the qere וּצְפוּנְךָ at v. 14, understood collectively as “your protected ones.”

**Scholarly interpretation**

Bazyliński’s abstract presents both as editorial judgments: the first is a conjecture, while the second prefers the qere reading. The English abstract does not expose the article’s full comparison of Hebrew manuscripts and ancient versions, so the item records the proposals without claiming which wording is earliest or original. The full article text is in Italian and was not reviewed.
