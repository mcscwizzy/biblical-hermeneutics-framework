## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 94 is a poetic appeal for divine justice, uses sustained parallelism, and stands after Psalm 93 in the Psalter; later Jewish practice assigns it to the fourth day of the week.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Jaffe argues from Psalm 94’s form, unusual diction, placement, and content that it can be read as the second half of Psalm 93, with both poems recalling the splitting of the sea. He proposes this as an explanation for Psalm 94’s place in the Jewish weekday-song cycle. The liturgical reception is distinct from evidence about the psalm’s original setting, and the proposed literary unity is not an established textual fact.
