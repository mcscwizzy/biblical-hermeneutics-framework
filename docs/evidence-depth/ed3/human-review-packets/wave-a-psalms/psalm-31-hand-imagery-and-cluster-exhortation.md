## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The speaker commits the spirit to YHWH’s hand and later says the speaker’s times are in that hand; the poem contrasts this with the enemy’s hand and hidden nets.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Botha reads Psalm 31 within Psalms 25-34 as a testimony of rescue that encourages a community to trust YHWH. He argues that the cluster’s central conflict is ethical and social, between humble and poor worshippers and arrogant persecutors in post-exilic Judah; this setting is his reconstruction.
