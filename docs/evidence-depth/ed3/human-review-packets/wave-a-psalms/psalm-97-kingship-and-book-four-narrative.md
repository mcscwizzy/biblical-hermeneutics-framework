## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

YHWH’s reign is announced, earth rejoices, heaven declares righteousness, Zion responds, and the righteous rejoice (vv. 1, 6–12); other gods bow (v. 7).

**Scholarly interpretation**

Hamilton reads Psalms 93–100 in their current Book IV sequence as a liturgical narrative of divine rule over cosmic and political rivals. He distinguishes this present arrangement from the origins and dates of individual psalms and does not establish a single historical enthronement festival.
