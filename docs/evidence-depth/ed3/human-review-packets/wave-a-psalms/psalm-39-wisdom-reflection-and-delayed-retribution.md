## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The speaker resolves to guard his speech while the wicked are present, describes human life as fleeting, and asks YHWH to turn his gaze away so he may smile again before he dies.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Botha reads the poem as a wisdom reflection on the apparent delay of retribution, in dialogue with the confident expectations of Psalms 34 and 37. He argues that the form of an individual illness supplication serves that reflection rather than documenting a specific sick person's cultic prayer. Other scholars read the illness language more directly, so the setting remains disputed.
