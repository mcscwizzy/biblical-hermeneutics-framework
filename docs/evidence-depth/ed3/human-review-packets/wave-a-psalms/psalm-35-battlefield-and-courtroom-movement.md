## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The petitioner asks YHWH to contend as warrior and judge; pursuit and defense language is followed by violent witnesses and false testimony, then renewed calls for vindication and public praise.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Simango treats recurring vows of praise and thanks as stanza markers and reads the opening section as foregrounding battle, the middle as foregrounding a lawsuit, and the closing section as combining both images. He notes an alternative classification as a royal prayer for international crisis, based on the military language. Genre and historical occasion therefore remain unsettled; the superscription and proposed Davidic correspondences do not establish authorship or one event.
