## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

After an opening plea and a section of praise and confidence, the poem closes by asking YHWH to save, bless, shepherd, and carry the people; its final movement remains petitionary.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Barus reads verses 1–5 as lament, verses 6–8 as praise, and the final verse as renewed prayer, producing a second mood change after the transition to praise. He notes alternatives that classify the final verse as praise or prayer. This is a literary reading and does not establish a particular temple rite or social setting.
