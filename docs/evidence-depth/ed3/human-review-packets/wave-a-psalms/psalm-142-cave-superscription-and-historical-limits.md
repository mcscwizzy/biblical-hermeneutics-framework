## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The heading associates Psalm 142 with David and a cave, but the poem itself does not name the cave, pursuer, or episode. Skinner compares its superscription and themes with the cave framing of Psalm 57.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Skinner proposes reading Psalm 142 as a thematic companion to Psalm 57; the cave heading may evoke more than one episode and should not be used to select a single historical event. This is an intertextual and editorial interpretation, not independent verification of a Davidic event.

Neither the heading nor the poem independently identifies one historical cave event.
