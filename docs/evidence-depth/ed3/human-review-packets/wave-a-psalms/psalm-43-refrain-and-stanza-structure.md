## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 43 has no separate superscription and closes with the same refrain about the downcast soul and hope in God that occurs twice immediately before it in Psalm 42. Its five verses form a short petition followed by renewed praise and hope.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Simango reads Psalms 42–43 as a unified individual lament divided into three stanzas by the shared refrain. He also notes that some ancient manuscript copies reportedly do not separate the two poems, citing Gerald H. Wilson rather than identifying particular witnesses. This item rests on the observable heading and refrain; it does not treat the manuscript report as independently verified or settle whether the poems were originally one composition. No cross-passage relationship edge is authored.
