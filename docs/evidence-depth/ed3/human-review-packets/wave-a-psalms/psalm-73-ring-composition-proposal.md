## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

**Scholarly interpretation**

Wong analyzes Psalm 73 as a ring composition using structural markers associated with Mary Douglas: he proposes a merged introduction and conclusion around a middle turning point, with chiastically arranged matching sections. The accessible abstract does not supply the full marker-by-marker case.
