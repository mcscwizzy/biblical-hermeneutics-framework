## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 33:6–9 pairs YHWH’s word and breath in creation with the sea gathered like a heap and the deep placed in storehouses; the surrounding lines say that YHWH spoke and it came to be.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Stoltz reads the parallel sea and deep as a merism for all waters under YHWH’s control and argues that the image may recall creation, the Reed Sea, and entry into Canaan. He notes a competing comparison with Job 38’s containment of the sea. These are literary resonances proposed in a master’s dissertation, not proof of dependence on Ugaritic poetry or a date for Psalm 33.
