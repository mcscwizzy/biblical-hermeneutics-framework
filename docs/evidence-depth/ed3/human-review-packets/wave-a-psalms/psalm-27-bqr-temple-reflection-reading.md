## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 27:4 pairs the desire to gaze on YHWH’s kindness with בקר in the phrase about YHWH’s temple; later lines repeat the desire to seek YHWH’s face and see divine goodness.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Botha argues that בקר should mean reflecting in the temple, as communion and Torah-related meditation, rather than inspecting a sacrificial offering or practicing divination. He bases the reading on parallelism, the two-part structure, and links with Psalms 16, 23, and 25-34.
