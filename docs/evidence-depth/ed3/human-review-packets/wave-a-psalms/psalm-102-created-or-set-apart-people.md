## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

In BHF numbering, Psalm 102:18b describes the people who praise Yah; many translations understand the participle from ברא as “created” and supply a future sense. Van Wolde parses the clause as a nominal description and reads the participle as a resulting state.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Van Wolde argues that the syntax of the clause and its present descriptive force favor “a people set apart—it praises Yah,” referring back to Zion’s inhabitants, rather than “a newly created people will praise Yah.” She notes that “create” is the standard interpretation and that the meaning of ברא is debated; her proposal is a translation argument, not a settled lexical consensus.
