## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

After describing divine knowledge and presence, Psalm 139:19–22 asks God to oppose the wicked and expresses the speaker's hatred of those who hate God; vv. 23–24 then ask God to search and lead the speaker.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Firth notes the live dispute over whether vv. 19–22 belong to the original poem and proposes a unified reading as a prayer of the accused. On this account, deliberate ambiguity allows innocent and guilty worshippers to hear the searching and imprecation differently. The article offers a reading, not proof that the integrity question is settled.
