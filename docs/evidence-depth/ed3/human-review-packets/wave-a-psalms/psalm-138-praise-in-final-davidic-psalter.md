## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 138 opens with a first-person vow to praise YHWH and bears a Davidic superscription. Botha treats the headings across Psalms 138–145 as significant for the final form of Book V, while explicitly cautioning that such headings do not by themselves prove Davidic authorship.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Botha argues that the Davidic superscriptions frame Psalms 138–145 as a final Davidic Psalter and portray David theologically as a dependent priest-king. This is a redactional reading of the Psalter’s received form; it is not evidence that the historical David composed Psalm 138 or that its original setting is recoverable.
