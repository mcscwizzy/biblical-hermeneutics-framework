## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 111's ten lines proceed through the Hebrew alphabet and praise YHWH's works, righteousness, and covenant faithfulness.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Prinsloo describes Psalms 111 and 112 as complete colon-based alphabetic acrostics and argues that the graphic technique probably presumes a culture of writing and reading, perhaps associated with scribal and wisdom schools. This is a qualified social inference from form, not evidence for a specific school, date, or original performance setting.
