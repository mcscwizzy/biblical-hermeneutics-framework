## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 16 names YHWH as the speaker’s allotted portion, describes counsel during the night, and closes with the path of life and enduring pleasures in YHWH’s presence.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Botha argues that these features make Psalm 16 a Torah-wisdom teaching composition, structurally and theologically related to Psalms 1, 19, and 23 within Psalms 15-24. He contrasts this with Hossfeld and Zenger’s interpretation as an individual supplication; his collection-history proposal is not treated as established chronology.
