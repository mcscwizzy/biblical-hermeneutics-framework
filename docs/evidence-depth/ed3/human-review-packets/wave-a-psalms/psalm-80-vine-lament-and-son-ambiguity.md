## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

A repeated restoration refrain frames the vine brought from Egypt and then devastated; the poem asks God to strengthen the man/son figure (vv. 4, 8–19 in common English numbering).

**Scholarly interpretation**

Streett describes a communal lament and treats vv. 15–18 as ambiguous: the vine, branch/son, and man may refer to the people or a leader/king. A royal reading is discussed but not made exclusive. Later reception is not projected into the original setting.
