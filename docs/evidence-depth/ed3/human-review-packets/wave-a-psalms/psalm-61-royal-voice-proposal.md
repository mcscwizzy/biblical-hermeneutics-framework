## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The petitioner seeks refuge in God's tent and beneath God's wings, then asks that the king's days be prolonged. The poem places this protective language and an explicit royal prayer together but does not directly identify the first-person voice as the king.

**Scholarly interpretation**

LeMon's publisher abstract says that an analysis of a bulla described as the ‘Hezekiah Seal’ strengthens the case for interpreting the voices in Psalms 61 and 63 as kings. This is reported as a scholarly proposal only: the abstract does not show the argument's steps, and this research did not independently inspect the bulla, its archaeological context, or the full article.

**Confidence / certainty / dispute**
