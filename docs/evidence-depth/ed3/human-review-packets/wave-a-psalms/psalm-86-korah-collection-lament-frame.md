## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 86 is headed as a prayer of David and is positioned between Psalms 84–85 and 87–88, whose headings and genres form a surrounding Korahite sequence.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Loader describes Psalms 84–88 as a 2+1+2 pattern: individual praise, collective lament, the central Davidic individual lament, Zion praise, and individual lament. He argues that this placement gives Psalm 86’s lament a wider contextual dimension of hope. The arrangement does not establish historical authorship by David, the identity of a historical speaker, or a composition date.
