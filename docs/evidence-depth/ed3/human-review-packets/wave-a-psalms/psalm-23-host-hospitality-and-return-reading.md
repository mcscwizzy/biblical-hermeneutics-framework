## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 23:5-6 moves from a prepared table, anointed head, and overflowing cup to a statement about the speaker’s future in YHWH’s house.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Arterbury and Bellinger argue that the host image is best understood against ancient hospitality customs and that this reading supports “I shall return to the house of the Lord.” The abstract is the accessible source; no consensus or exclusive translation is claimed.
