## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The speaker names flesh, bones, wounds, bent posture, heart, eyes, mouth, and feet in the lament; a plea for help at the opening is answered by a renewed plea at the close.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Human interprets the distributed body-part imagery as a portrayal of the sufferer's injured and sick body and reads the final plea for YHWH's presence as a prayer for wholeness and restored relations. He cautions that poetic body images may be literal, figurative, or symbolic; the item does not treat the poem as evidence of a specific disease or medical cause.
