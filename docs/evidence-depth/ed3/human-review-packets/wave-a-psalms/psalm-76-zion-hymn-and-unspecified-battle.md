## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The poem names Judah, Israel, Salem, and Zion and praises YHWH’s victory, but it does not identify a human opponent, ruler, or datable battle. BHF Psalm 76:1–12 corresponds to the dissertation’s Psalm 76:2–13 numbering, which counts the heading.

**Scholarly interpretation**

In his passage-specific discussion, Smith reads Psalm 76 as a generalized divine-warrior and Zion hymn and places it within the editorial grouping of Psalms 74–76. He does not identify a single historical battle as the poem’s referent. His larger exilic-crisis setting is a literary-historical reconstruction, not a date established by this poem alone.
