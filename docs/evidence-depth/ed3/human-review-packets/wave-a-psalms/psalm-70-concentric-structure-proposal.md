## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

In BHF’s five body verses, the opening and closing lines ask God to hurry to help, while the middle verse turns toward those who seek God and rejoice.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Auffret’s abstract says he proposes a concentric pattern around his verse 4, with verses 3 and 5 corresponding and then verses 2 and 6. This numbering counts the superscription as verse 1; the center maps to BHF Psalm 70:3, the adjacent pair to BHF 70:2 and 70:4, and the outer pair to 70:1 and 70:5. The full French analysis was not accessible, so this is an abstract-level report, not a judgment that the structure is settled.
