## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 40:2-4 recounts being drawn from danger and given a new song; Jassen identifies language and structure in this passage as a model used in 4Q160 3–5 ii 1–4.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Jassen argues that Psalm 40 moves from remembered divine aid to a current petition, whereas the author of 4Q160 combines those functions in one prayer while retaining language and structure from Psalm 40:2–4. This is a proposal about later literary reuse, not a claim that 4Q160 preserves a variant biblical reading.

**Confidence / certainty / dispute**

low / plausible / interpretive_uncertainty
