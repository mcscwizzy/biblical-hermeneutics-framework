## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

After recalling what the community has heard and seen, Psalm 48 directs hearers to walk around Zion, count its towers, and consider its rampart and buildings before telling the next generation.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Steiner distinguishes a proposed earlier layer that grounds confidence in the city's apparent security from an exilic layer that turns this physical space into a remembered and narrated testimony of divine care. He also notes that scholars propose widely different historical occasions and that the poem does not name one specific attack. The layering is a literary-historical model, not an independently established event sequence.
