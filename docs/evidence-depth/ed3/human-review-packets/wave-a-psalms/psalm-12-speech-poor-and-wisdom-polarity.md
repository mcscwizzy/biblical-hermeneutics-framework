## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The complaint depicts neighbors speaking falsely and boasting; the speaker contrasts this speech with YHWH's tested words and the promise that YHWH will rise because of the poor and needy's groaning.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Botha argues that Psalm 12 participates in a wisdom-shaped literary response to Proverbs 30:1–14 within Psalms 9–14: arrogant speech exploits the poor while YHWH's reliable words promise protection. He proposes a Persian-period horizon for the final form but says the psalm cannot be pinned to a specific date; the literary relationship and social reconstruction remain proposals.
