## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 99 repeatedly proclaims YHWH’s kingship and holiness, locates the divine king at Zion in verse 2, and recalls Moses, Aaron, and Samuel.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Hamilton notes a proposal that Psalm 99’s unique Zion reference entered the YHWH-mālak group at a relatively late stage. He offers a final-form alternative: after Psalm 98’s call to sing, Psalm 99 names Zion as the congregation’s location and emphasizes the kingship that the sequence celebrates. In his reading, the placement does not determine when the psalm was composed or incorporated; he explicitly leaves the chronology open.
