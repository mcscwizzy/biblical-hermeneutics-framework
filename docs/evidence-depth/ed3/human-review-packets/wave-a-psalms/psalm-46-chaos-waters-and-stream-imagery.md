## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 46:2–5 depicts mountains and waters in upheaval, then names a river whose channels gladden God's city and says God is in its midst.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Schäder reads the contrast spatially: the raging waters and hostile nations belong to a destabilized outside, while the city's stream and divine presence mark Zion as an ordered center. The proposal helps explain the poem's geography but does not identify a physical river or prove a particular cultic setting.
