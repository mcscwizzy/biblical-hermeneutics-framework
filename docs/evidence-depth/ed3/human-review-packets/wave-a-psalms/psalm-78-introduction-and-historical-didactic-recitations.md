## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 78:1-8 addresses hearers and calls the coming generation to learn; vv. 9-72 recount episodes of Israel’s history through repeated patterns of failure, divine response, and remembered provision.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Kim and van Rooy propose an introduction in vv. 1-8 followed by two historical-didactic recitals in vv. 9-39 and 40-72. They explicitly note that scholars disagree over the Psalm’s structure, genre, date, and setting, with proposals ranging from the tenth century to the postexilic period; this item assigns no composition date.

**Confidence / certainty / dispute**

medium / plausible / major_scholarly_disagreement

**Historical and compositional qualifications**
