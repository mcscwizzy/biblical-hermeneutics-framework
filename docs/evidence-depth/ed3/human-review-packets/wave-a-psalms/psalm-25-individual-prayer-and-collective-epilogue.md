## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Psalm 25 is an alphabetic acrostic prayer with first-person petitions and reflections through vv. 1–21; v. 22 shifts to an explicit plea to redeem Israel. The final verse sits outside the preceding individual address and sequence.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Human reads vv. 1–21 primarily as an individual lament and describes v. 22 as an epilogue that extends the worshipper’s experience to Israel. He allows that the verse may be a later addition, while other structural descriptions place the boundary differently. The text’s acrostic form does not by itself establish its date or original use.
