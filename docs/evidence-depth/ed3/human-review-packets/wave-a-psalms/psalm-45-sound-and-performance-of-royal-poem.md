## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The speaker announces a good composition for the king and likens his tongue to a skilled scribe's stylus or pen, making speech and crafted writing the poem's opening image.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Wendland analyzes the parallel lineation, rhythmic balance, tricola, and recurring alliteration as features that contribute to Psalm 45's rhetorical force and make it suited to oral articulation. His Chewa examples concern translation practice; they do not prescribe one rendering for every language, and the proposed public court performance is not independently established.
