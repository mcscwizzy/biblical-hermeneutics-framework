## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The Masoretic wording in Psalm 110:4 uses עַל־דִּבְרָתִי מַלְכִּי־צֶדֶק. Ancient translations and modern interpreters construe the phrase differently; the Hebrew text alone does not make the familiar English phrase “order of Melchizedek” unambiguous.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Corley reviews readings that understand the phrase as “according to the order of Melchizedek” as well as alternatives involving “my word,” “manner,” or “because of.” The disputed wording shapes proposals about how royal and priestly authority are joined in the poem. The evidence does not establish a precise composition date or a single cultic office.
