## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The petitioner recalls being taught from youth, asks not to be abandoned in old age, and links that request to proclaiming God's might to the next generation.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Liess interprets the psalm's lifespan language as continuity between remembered divine care and future communal witness: the older petitioner still has a task of praise and proclamation. This literary reading does not identify a historical individual or show that every older person held the same social role.
