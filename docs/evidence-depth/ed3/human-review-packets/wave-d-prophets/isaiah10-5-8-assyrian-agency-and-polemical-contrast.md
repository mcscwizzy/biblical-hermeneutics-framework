## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Isaiah 10:5–6 calls Assyria a rod and staff in YHWH’s hand and commissions it to plunder; verses 7–8 say Assyria intends to destroy many nations and boasts that its commanders are like kings.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Aster reads the contrast as a polemical limit on Assyria’s power: the empire may despoil subject peoples, but its own claim to destroy nations exceeds the commission represented in the text. He relates the language to Assyrian imperial claims and distinguishes tribute extracted from vassal states from provincial taxation. This is Aster’s literary-historical reading, not an independent Assyrian confirmation of the oracle’s theology or a settled composition date.
