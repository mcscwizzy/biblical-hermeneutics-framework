## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The Masoretic Text begins verse 8 with a question about No-Amon and then names Cush, Egypt, Put, and Libya in verse 9. Grütter compares this wording with the Old Greek, pre-Hexaplaric evidence, and Qumran 4Q385a, whose text and pronouns differ in ways relevant to the city reference.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Grütter argues that the Septuagint and 4Q385a support an earlier one-city reading about Nineveh, while the Masoretic form presents the familiar comparison with No-Amon/Thebes. She treats this as a text-critical explanation for the passage’s wording and topographical difficulty, not as archaeological evidence about Thebes or a settled consensus.
