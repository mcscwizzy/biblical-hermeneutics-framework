## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Ndoga reports that the Masoretic wording in Ezekiel 22:25 uses the Hebrew word for “prophet,” though several English translations render “princes.” He notes the distinct mention of prophets in verse 28 and discusses the Septuagint rendering and a proposed emendation to “princes.”

**Scholarly interpretation**

Ndoga finds “princes” contextually plausible because the predatory conduct in verse 25 fits political leaders, while also recognizing that the Hebrew “prophets” reading avoids an unexplained repetition in verse 28 only if the surrounding literary pattern is read differently. The item preserves this as a textual and interpretive question; it does not claim that an extant Hebrew witness reads “princes.”
