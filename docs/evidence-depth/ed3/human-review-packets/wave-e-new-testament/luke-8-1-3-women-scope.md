## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Luke 8:1–3 names Mary Magdalene, Joanna, and Susanna, then refers to καὶ ἕτεραι πολλαί and αἵτινες διηκόνουν αὐτοῖς ἐκ τῶν ὑπαρχόντων αὐταῖς. The text’s sequence and Greek syntax raise questions about whether the healing description and the service clause apply to all of the women or only to some.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Blajer argues that ἕτεραι (“others”) most likely distinguishes an additional group from the three named women, so the text need not portray all Jesus’ female companions as previously healed. He also reports disagreement about whether “who provided for them” refers to all the women or only to the unnamed group, and says the syntax is not decisive. His reading should not be used to infer that every woman was wealthy or that service necessarily meant financial patronage.
