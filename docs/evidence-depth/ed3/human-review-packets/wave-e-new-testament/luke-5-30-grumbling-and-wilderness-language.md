## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

At Luke 5:30 the Greek narrator uses ἐγόγγυζον for the Pharisees’ response to Jesus eating with tax collectors and sinners. Tuohy notes that the related verb appears in Septuagint accounts of Israel’s wilderness complaints, including Exodus 15–16 and Numbers 14.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Tuohy suggests that the vocabulary may make the criticism resemble Israel’s wilderness grumbling, now directed at Jesus’ meal. This is a possible scriptural resonance rather than a demonstrated quotation or certain authorial allusion; the narrative’s immediate focus remains the dispute over Jesus’ meal companions.
