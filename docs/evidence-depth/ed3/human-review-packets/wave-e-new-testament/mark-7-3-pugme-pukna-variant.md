## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Mark 7:3 contains the difficult Greek expression πυγμῇ in the critical editions named in Burkett’s abstract. His 2019 article proposes the variant πυκνά, meaning ‘repeatedly’; the footnote displayed by the publisher lists א and W among its witnesses. No manuscript images were consulted, and the original reading remains unresolved.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Burkett argues that πυκνά is preferable and says this sense fits descriptions of repeated hand-rinsing in later rabbinic sources. Because only the abstract and a displayed apparatus note were accessible, this item does not assess his full argument or infer a uniform handwashing practice in the first-century setting of Mark.
