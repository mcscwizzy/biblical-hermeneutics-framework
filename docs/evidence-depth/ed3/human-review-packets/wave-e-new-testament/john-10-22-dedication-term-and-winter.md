## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

John 10:22 calls the occasion τὰ ἐγκαίνια and explicitly adds that it was winter. The Greek verse does not use the later English label “Hanukkah” as a transliterated festival name.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Förster argues that τὰ ἐγκαίνια means a dedication celebration rather than functioning as a unique proper name. On this reading, the winter notice helps distinguish the festival from other temple-dedication celebrations and identifies Hanukkah. This proposal is reported from the abstract only; no alternate dedication or audience conclusion is adopted here.
