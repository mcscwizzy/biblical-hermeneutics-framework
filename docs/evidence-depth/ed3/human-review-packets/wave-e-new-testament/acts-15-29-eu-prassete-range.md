## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

Acts 15:28–29 presents the council’s decision and four abstentions; the letter ends by saying that recipients who keep from these matters will εὖ πράξετε. The phrase has both a well-being/prosperity sense and an acting-well sense in Greek usage.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Moxon’s selected corpus finds the well-being sense more commonly attested, while commendable or correct action remains a genuine, less frequent sense. Epistolary and rhetorical context can affect the choice, but the phrase does not by itself decide the decree’s meaning or purpose; Moxon leaves the intended force open.
