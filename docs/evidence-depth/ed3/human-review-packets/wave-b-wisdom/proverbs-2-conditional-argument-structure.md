## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The chapter begins with a conditional address (“my son, if …”) in vv. 1–4 and develops successive consequences through the remainder of the poem, ending with the ways of the upright and wicked in vv. 20–22.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Loader argues that v. 20 is the capstone of the whole conditional argument, because the apparent break after v. 11 is syntactically weak and the material in vv. 12–19 continues the prior construction. He contrasts this with Fox’s proposed break after v. 11; the structural question remains open.
