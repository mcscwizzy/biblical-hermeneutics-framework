## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The Masoretic text includes Proverbs 16:1–3, which are absent from the Old Greek, and places material corresponding to Old Greek Proverbs 15:27a, 28a, 29a–b at 16:6–9. Macchi notes that 4QProvb preserves only part of this section and supports the MT order where it overlaps.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Macchi argues that a proto-Masoretic editor reshaped a Hebrew form underlying the Old Greek by relocating several lines and composing MT Proverbs 16:1–3. In his reading, this yields a section on divine sovereignty and gathers the material that leads into the royal sayings of 16:10–15. Translation freedom and other text-history models remain alternatives; the article does not establish a precise date for the proposed editing.
