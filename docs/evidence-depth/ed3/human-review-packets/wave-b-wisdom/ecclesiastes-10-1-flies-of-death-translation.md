## Claim under review

**Primary textual observation**

The Hebrew expression is literally rendered “flies of death”; the following odor verb is singular although “flies” is plural, and Whybray notes another singular verb and a phrase that may be glosses.

**Scholarly interpretation**

Whybray lists “dead,” “poisonous/lethal,” and “dying” flies as possible senses. He regards the verse as probably textually corrupt but says its general point is fairly clear; the record reports his alternatives and does not resolve the Hebrew text.
