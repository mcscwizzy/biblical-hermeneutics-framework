# Post-session Questions

Ask these questions after all tasks. Read each as written and accept a short
answer, “not sure,” “nothing,” or no answer without pressure.

1. **“Was there anything you noticed or learned that made you want to explore the passage further?”**
2. **“Which part of BHF was most confusing?”**
3. **“What would you change?”**
4. “Was anything confusing because of the passage itself, the way BHF explained it, or the way the interface worked?”
5. “Is there anything else about finding or checking the evidence that you want to tell me?”

Do not assume the participant discovered something, found the experience useful,
or wants to continue studying. Record an explicit no or no response accurately.
Separate a direct quotation from a facilitator paraphrase, and use verbatim
quotes only when separate quote consent was given.
