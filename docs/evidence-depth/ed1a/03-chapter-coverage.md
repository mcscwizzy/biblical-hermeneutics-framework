# ED-1A chapter coverage by book and chapter

The companion [`chapter-coverage.csv`](chapter-coverage.csv) contains **1,189 rows**, one for every canonical chapter. It gives exact chapter-level coverage state, eligible item and unclassified placements, scope counts, source traceability, typed domain indicators, current section assignment, and NE-10 bridge endpoints. [`summary.json`](summary.json) contains the reproduction baseline, audit methods, CKL/source/lexical inventories, domain totals, and all 66 book summaries.

A book-level domain cell below is the number of chapters in that book with at least one source-traceable CKL record of that typed domain. It is not a count of unique records or a depth score. Empty chapter views are not scholarly absence claims.

## Findings from the full chapter rows

- **132 chapters** have no eligible CKL item: Psalms 74; 2 Chronicles 14; Proverbs 12; Ezekiel 9; 1 Kings 6; 2 Kings 6; Numbers 4; 1 Chronicles 3; Luke 3; Ecclesiastes 1. Filter `coverage_category=no_eligible_ckl_material` in the CSV for each exact chapter.
- **68 contextual**, **3 entity-only**, and **1 unknown** chapter are distinct from no-material chapters. The unknown row is Obadiah 1; its invalid anchor is described in the inventory report.
- **394 chapters** have at least one projected CKL item without an assigned section; the CSV reports exact per-chapter counts. Review exact item identity before calling any one placement a reader-visible loss.
- Domain tables distinguish lexical claims from literary claims. A lexical database lookup does not appear as a passage-specific CKL lexical indicator.
- Historical snapshots differ by commit and projector. This document reports only the regenerated baseline at the ED-1A branch base; the comparison is in [01-current-inventory.md](01-current-inventory.md).
