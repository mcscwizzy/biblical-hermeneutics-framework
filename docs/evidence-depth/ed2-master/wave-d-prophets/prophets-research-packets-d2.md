## Nahum 3:8–9 — Nineveh and No-Amon

- **Existing CKL material:** Nahum 3 already has substantial source-backed and passage-specific content, including the received comparison with No-Amon/Thebes. This addition does not rewrite that account or alter existing place-identification judgments.
- **Research question:** Does the Hebrew form compare Nineveh with a second city, or does ancient textual evidence support a one-city reading?
- **Verified source:** Nesina Grütter, “A Tale of One City (Nah 3:8–9): A Text-critical Solution for an Often Discussed Problem Provided by a Reading Preserved in the Septuagint,” *Journal of Septuagint and Cognate Studies* 50 (2017), 160–175, Wevers Prize Paper 2016. The complete article in the Göttingen IOSCS issue PDF was inspected.
- **Textual observation:** The Masoretic Text opens verse 8 with a question about No-Amon and names Cush, Egypt, Put, and Libya in verse 9. Grütter compares this wording with the Old Greek, pre-Hexaplaric revisions, and Qumran 4Q385a; the witnesses differ in the forms and pronouns relevant to the city reference.
- **Scholarly interpretation:** Grütter argues that the Septuagint and 4Q385a support an earlier one-city reading about Nineveh, while the Masoretic Text presents the familiar comparison with No-Amon/Thebes.
- **Uncertainty:** This is a low-confidence, disputed reconstruction, not a consensus replacement for the Masoretic reading. It does not establish the archaeological identity of No-Amon, a site claim, or the oracle’s composition date.
- **Record:** `nahum-3-8-9-one-city-text-critical-proposal`.
- **Reader relevance:** Makes the textual basis for a possible “tale of one city” visible while preserving the received text and its dominant interpretation as a live alternative.
