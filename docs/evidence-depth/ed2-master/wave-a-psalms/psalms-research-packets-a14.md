## Psalm 110:4 — the Melchizedek phrase

- **Existing CKL material:** Psalm 110 appears in broad kingship, messianic, and New Testament reception records. The existing held NE-10 candidate linking Psalm 110 and Acts 2 remains held and unchanged.
- **Research question:** Does the Hebrew phrase in Psalm 110:4 unambiguously mean “according to the order of Melchizedek”?
- **Verified source:** Jeremy Corley, “Psalm 110 (109) and Israelite Royal Ritual,” *Salmanticensis* 64, no. 1 (2017), 41–71, DOI 10.36576/summa.45851, especially pp. 53–54. The full open PDF was inspected.
- **Textual observation:** The Masoretic phrase עַל־דִּבְרָתִי מַלְכִּי־צֶדֶק is rendered in more than one way; ancient translations and modern scholarship do not eliminate the Hebrew syntactic and lexical ambiguity.
- **Scholarly interpretation:** Corley reviews “according to the order of Melchizedek” alongside alternatives involving “my word,” “manner,” or “because of.” The wording informs proposals about the relation of kingship and priesthood, but does not by itself identify a particular historical office or setting.
- **Uncertainty:** The phrase and the poem’s historical setting remain disputed. Composition date is not inferred from the superscription or later reception.
- **Record:** `psalm-110-order-of-melchizedek-debate`.
- **Reader relevance:** Readers can see why translations differ and distinguish the Hebrew poem’s unresolved wording from Hebrews 7’s later reuse.
