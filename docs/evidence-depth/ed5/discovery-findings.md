# ED-5 discovery findings

## Verified opportunities

1. **Completed-empty CKL coverage lacked a passage-level explanation.** The
   overview exposed “No linked records returned” in topic cards. That wording
   did not explicitly separate BHF's current coverage from the historical
   importance of the passage, nor point to an existing study action. The
   reproduced 1 Kings 2 census row is a valid no-eligible-material case.
2. **Common research terminology had no shared quick reference in the
   Narrative Explorer overview.** The selected journeys include textual
   witnesses, ancient translations, lexical uncertainty, redaction,
   reception, literary structure, historical reconstruction, and proposed
   intertextual relationships. Existing records preserve these distinctions,
   but a reader encountering the terms had to rely on the record or outside
   knowledge.

## Checks that did not justify a content change

- Exact CKL identifiers, passage applicability, provenance IDs, review states,
  certainty, and dispute metadata were kept as acceptance conditions; no
  record was retitled or reclassified to improve apparent engagement.
- Additional sourced records already have separate progressive disclosure
  and duplicate suppression. No new defect was reproduced in the selected
  Genesis 12 / 1 Corinthians 11:3 contracts.
- Source details already retain their source IDs and bibliographic fields.
  Existing tests cover visible locators, review notices, and source disclosure.
  Current browser execution was unavailable, so a new layout/source defect was
  not established.
- Six categories remain unchanged. Chapter Location quick glance, its source
  and uncertainty details, and geography services were not changed.
- No eligible evidence in 1 Kings 2 is not evidence that the passage lacks
  historical significance. The new empty notice states that distinction.

These are heuristic findings from repository inspection and scripted
projections, not participant observations.
