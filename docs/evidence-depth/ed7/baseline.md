## Reader baseline

Before changing the page, browser captures were taken from the ED-6 base for
the ED-5/ED-6 selected passages at desktop width and for Genesis 6, Psalm 82,
and 1 Kings 2 at 390px and 320px. Ten prescribed journeys were inspected:
Genesis 6, Genesis 12, Psalm 82, Deuteronomy 32:8–9, Psalm 145:13, Jonah
1:17→Matthew 12:40, 1 Corinthians 11:3, 1 John 3:19–24, John 10:22, and
1 Kings 2.

The initial view retained six equal topic cards, displayed category-level
lookup states and record counts, and used labels such as “Source lookup
complete.” The 1 Kings 2 empty state said “Research coverage in BHF” and
included the count of linked records even though no passage-specific item was
eligible. Detailed “Additional sourced records” could also repeat less
relevant items below the categories. The baseline images are in
[`screenshots/before/`](screenshots/before/).

## Scripture and source baseline limits

Baseline display counts are counts of cards and labels rendered by the page;
the numeric API projection counts in `screenshots/journey-metrics.json` count
retrieved records and are not reader-visible-card counts. Neither is a measure
of reader understanding. Source citations and linked passages already existed;
this work changes their presentation and promotes no relationship solely
because Scripture references overlap.
