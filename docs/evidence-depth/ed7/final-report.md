# ED-7 Final Report

## Outcome

ED-7 changes the Narrative Explorer's initial view from query and workflow
metadata to a reader-first passage experience. It preserves six topic areas,
chapter Location quick glance, exact-source disclosure, uncertainty, and
directional navigation through existing verified connections. Two source
rechecked discoveries are eligible for featured presentation; ordinary
passages with no eligible finding get a plain and truthful empty state.

The audit covers all 99 existing ED-3 queue records. It found 28 claims
supported with qualifications and 71 whose support could not be sufficiently
verified. It found no new correction requirement and no item unsupported by
the available evidence. It did not classify any record as unqualifiedly
supported. The second analytical pass was performed by the same GPT-6 agent,
not an independent model, human reviewer, or peer reviewer.

## Governance preserved

All 687 CKL objects, the 44 verified bridges, 7 held candidates, protected
NE-9C/NE-10 decisions, existing source identities, draft/review histories, and
the 99-record human-review queue were preserved. ED-7 adds a presentation
policy separate from CKL states. No research was deleted, no evidence was
invented, and no approval state changed. The ED-6 participant study remains
independent and ready for use.
