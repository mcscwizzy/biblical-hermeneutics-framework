# Withheld Research Report

## Result

The 71 audited records placed in **withheld from reader promotion** all have
outcome `source_inaccessible_or_insufficiently_verified`. This is an evidence
access/verification limit; it is not a finding that those records are false,
unsupported, or unimportant. No ED-7 row was classified unsupported, and no
new correction was required.

The item-level reasons, cited sources, exact access limits, unresolved
objections, stable CKL IDs, source IDs, and packet checksums are preserved in
[`source-verification-results.json`](source-verification-results.json). For 69
records, no cited publication text was freshly inspected in this pass; the
audit reconciled their existing CKL entry, ED-3 inventory, queue row, and
packet. Other rows had limited material such as a publisher abstract, partial
excerpt, or a checked edition text without the cited scholarly argument or
primary apparatus. The precise inspected materials are recorded per item.

## Reader effect

These records no longer appear as a promoted finding or as a technical “extra
sourced records” list in the overview. They remain in the canonical research
library, source and evidence relations, passage anchors, and maintainer audit
records. The Canonical Browser and research tools remain the paths for deeper
search. No data, source, evidence relation, or Scripture relationship was
deleted to achieve the reader simplification.

The 26 audited items assigned “available on demand” remain accessible in
topic-level exploration. The two featured items have explicit source notes,
limitations, and natural-language qualifications. No withheld item was
replaced with newly invented evidence.
