# Commentary next steps

Commentary v1.0 is the frozen baseline. Future work should preserve strict
scripture-anchor retrieval, evidence grounding, and validator behavior.

Possible follow-up work:

- Expand CKL coverage, especially chapters currently classified as `THIN` or
  `DATA_GAP`.
- Add carefully sourced archaeology and material-culture resources.
- Add additional language and textual resources.
- Integrate chapter commentary into the BHF reading and study UI.
- Add translation-comparison integration.
- Visualize evidence anchors, categories, and uncertainty.
- Establish a human feedback and review loop.

These are planning items only; they are not part of the v1.0 release.
