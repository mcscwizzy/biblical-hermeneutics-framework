# NE-6 Cosmic Geography Presentation

## Conceptual scope

The Divine Council & Cosmic Geography section presents source-grounded textual and interpretive material. It is not a physical map layer and does not depict a literal map of heavenly realms. Records enter the section only through the established source-specific evidence type, authored identity, and passage-anchor rules. Titles and descriptions are not used as classification keywords.

When projected metadata qualifies, the reader uses these authored role labels:

- Sacred-space interpretation
- Sacred-space guarding context
- Heavenly assembly language
- Divine assembly comparison
- Ancient cosmology comparison
- Divine beings with disputed interpretation

The exact source rule determines the label. A mountain, garden, temple, throne, sea, or reference to heaven does not qualify by itself. A physical place record remains separate even when a source also supports a conceptual sacred-space reading.

## Textual and interpretive distinctions

The evidence detail retains original source identity, passage anchors, relationship labels, source citations, and any author-provided interpretation state or dispute. The presentation keeps apart:

1. What the biblical passage says.
2. Physical geographical context and the uncertainty of identifying places.
3. Ancient cultural or literary comparisons.
4. A proposed symbolic or sacred-space reading.
5. Later biblical reception.
6. Later Jewish or Christian interpretation.
7. Scholarly uncertainty and disagreement.

The UI states that comparative material does not by itself establish borrowing, quotation, or dependence, and that symbolic readings are interpretations rather than archaeological findings. It adds no doctrinal conclusion. Source-specific warnings and the original one-way relationship labels are retained; the UI does not create reciprocal edges.

## Geographic pilots

Genesis 2 presents garden-presence and Eden-as-sacred-space material as interpretation, while the Eden catalog point remains physically unidentified. Genesis 3 shows cherubim only as qualified guarding/sacred-space evidence; that connection does not locate Eden archaeologically. Genesis 6 keeps the sons-of-God material visibly disputed and does not place heavenly beings at a physical site.

Deuteronomy 32 retains its textual-witness evidence without forcing nations language into a settled cosmic territorial map. Psalm 82 separates the passage's explicit assembly-language evidence from Ugaritic comparison. Exodus 3 distinguishes Midian and uncertain Horeb/Sinai physical geography from Divine Presence themes; the current CKL set lacks passage-specific sacred-space evidence here. That gap remains open for future CKL curation.

## Navigation

The existing NE-5C trail displays authored passage, source, subject, and related-text links where the source provides them. Original IDs and labels remain intact. Directionality, the three-hop limit, breadcrumb history, browser Back/Forward behavior, and return-to-origin selection and translation are preserved. No missing evidence or relationship is generated to make a trail complete.
