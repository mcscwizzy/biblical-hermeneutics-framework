## Held and excluded decisions

- **Held (53):** includes the Gezer and Hazor book-level edges. Although 1 Kings 9:15–17 names the sites, the exact 1 Kings endpoint’s root scope is 9:1–9; no chapter-level scope was inherited. The dating and identification of excavated Iron Age structures remain debated. The Qumran settlement-to-book, site-to-cave, and Great Isaiah Scroll/Cave 1 provenance edges were held because the attached records do not establish those specific archaeological or manuscript claims. Davidic and Christological links were held where the generic edge did not identify a specific reception text. The divine-name word-study matrix was held because a common proof text does not make YHWH, Elohim, Adonai, Kyrios, and Theos interchangeable. [Keimer, “The Solomonic Gate at Gezer in Its Context”](https://openscholar.huji.ac.il/sites/default/files/jjar/files/keimer_2021_jjar_1_68-101.pdf), [Oxford Academic, “The Archaeology of Memory: Solomon, History, and Biblical Representation”](https://doi.org/10.1093/0195177967.003.0005).
- **Excluded (360):** generic blank-note `related-concept` edges, broad word-study pairings, and concept tags without a distinct passage-specific claim. Exclusion applies to the authored relationship only; canonical CKL source objects remain unchanged.
