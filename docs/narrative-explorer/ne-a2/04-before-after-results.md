# NE-A2 before/after results

## Baseline and scope

The feature branch is based directly on `origin/master` commit `a320e0adda1bda0403bdcb2c82cb8b5107d9a4cd` (`Add NE-A1 CKL coverage audit`). The historical NE-A1 files remain unchanged. The pre-change baseline is 687 CKL objects, 1,189 canonical chapters, 985 chapters with passage-specific evidence, 1,892 unassigned item-chapter placements in 591 chapters, 132 chapters without eligible CKL material, one unresolved Obadiah scope issue, and 4,061 authored top-level object relationships.

The A2 full chapter census used the same committed CKL JSON and read-only study database snapshot as the baseline, forced the JSON CKL backend, and passed census validation for all 1,189 chapters. The API parity capture compared 26 chapter and verse-range scenarios with three repetitions each. Separate live map lookup calls were performed for 16 census sample chapters. Map results are not claimed for all 1,189 chapters.

## Exact projection changes

The full census found **509 distinct previously unassigned stable items across 869 item-chapter placements** newly assigned by the verified type and authored-child-link rules. This reduces the residual queue from 1,892 to **1,023 placements** and from 839 to **330 distinct unassigned IDs**. Chapters with at least one remaining unassigned placement decrease from 591 to 394. The new section memberships are 576 in `archaeology_ancient_history` and 567 in `themes_biblical_connections`.

Across the whole projection, 604 distinct item IDs and 1,140 item-chapter placements have a changed section membership, including already-classified parent items that aggregate eligible child sections. This adds 1,143 section memberships: Archaeology & Ancient History grows from 2,475 to 3,051 item memberships (497 to 541 chapters); Themes & Biblical Connections grows from 5,588 to 6,155 (851 to 909 chapters). The other section totals do not change. Chapters with any section membership rise from 1,043 to 1,050; 445 chapter rows have at least one changed section count. These are presentation memberships, not new evidence or upgraded evidence-quality classifications.

The 330 remaining IDs are not auto-routed. The curation queue includes all 1,023 residual placements with stable item IDs, record kind/type, passage anchors, authored categories, source references, and available certainty/dispute/review metadata. All residual records have resolvable source IDs and bibliographic parent metadata. They require human classification or a narrower policy; no title/summary/keyword heuristic was introduced. The queue does not label these placements as missing evidence.

## Relationship changes

The 4,061 authored top-level relationship inventory has 1,446 original labels. The exact-label policy classifies 113 top-level edges across 14 labels; 3,948 remain `uncategorized`. The classified types are `thematic_relationship` (104 edges), `narrative_parallel` (1), `direct_quotation` (1), `later_canonical_reception` (5), and `interpretive_association` (2). The table also classifies two evidence-level `canonical-theme` edges, for 115 existing relationship records affected in total. 49 mapped top-level edges are book-scoped and remain excluded by the current projection guard. Passage-anchor eligibility still governs the remaining edges.

The 98 authored reciprocal pairs and 3,865 one-way top-level edges are unchanged. For the five explicitly later-reception labels, the temporal layer changes from its previous state to `later_reception`. No reverse edges are invented. Original labels, source/target IDs and order, notes, weights, basis, relationship mode, and inference/directness distinction are preserved. Labels such as `related-concept`, `institutional-anchor`, `historical-layer`, `canonical-fulfillment`, and `later-sanctuary-development` remain uncategorized under the documented policy.

## Acts 7 and map coverage

The reproducible text-first defect and additive explicit-reference correction are documented in [03-acts7-map-investigation.md](03-acts7-map-investigation.md). Acts 7 gains the Nile record anchored at Acts 7:19; the record's original source relation and uncertainty are retained, and its coordinates remain non-displayable. The same retrieval rule exposes the anchored Negeb record in Joshua 1. Across the 16 sampled live map lookups, 16 partial lookups become complete. The API parity set contains 24 scenarios whose map coverage metadata changes from partial/unknown to completed lookup; the other two were already complete. No claim is made about map-service lookups across the whole canon.

## Coverage and mandatory safeguards

The census evidence-quality categories remain exactly: 985 `passage_specific_evidence`, 132 `no_eligible_ckl_material`, 68 `contextual_coverage`, 3 `entity_only_coverage`, and 1 `incomplete_or_unknown`. Total eligible placements remain 12,662. The 132 no-material chapters and unresolved Obadiah issue remain.

Regression tests retain the existing NE-5A source-specific rules: Genesis 1 comparisons retain their comparative status; Genesis 2-3 Eden/cherubim retain supported sacred-space labels without physical geolocation; Genesis 6 sons-of-God views remain disputed and cross-reference leakage remains filtered; Deuteronomy 32 manuscript evidence remains distinct from a forced council interpretation; Psalm 82 council and Ugaritic evidence remain separate identities; Exodus 3 Presence Theme/Divine Presence remain under Themes. No generic cosmic-geography assignments were added for worldview, presence, mountain, temple, kingship, or glory. Negative controls verify that unclear `canonical-fulfillment` relationships remain uncategorized and unsupported locations do not gain displayable coordinates.

## API compatibility and performance

The projection response schema remains `1.0`, and the Narrative Explorer feature flag remains off by default. In the 26-case paired API comparison, no existing item or connection was removed. Existing item fields changed only in `section_ids`; existing connection fields changed only in `connection_type`. The five later-reception temporal updates are tracked by stable edge ID in the full source ledger; none appeared as a separate field change in these 26 API scenarios. Unaffected items and connections remained identical. Existing IDs, sources, Scripture anchors, confidence/certainty, dispute/review/interpretation states, edge direction, basis, and navigation targets passed preservation assertions. The only new projected items are the two explicitly passage-linked map records, with their two explicit anchor connections. Coverage changes are limited to the completed map lookup state.

The local paired measurements use three repetitions per scenario. Median of the 26 per-case medians increased from 1,208.014 ms to 1,257.285 ms (+4.08%); the mean of per-case means increased from 1,543.158 ms to 1,608.528 ms (+4.2%). Median response size changed from 222,570 to 222,776 bytes; total bytes across the 26 responses increased from 6,576,655 to 6,666,364 (+1.36%). These local timings include the extra explicit-reference retrieval and are not production latency guarantees. The observed increase is modest relative to the previously documented NE-5B improvement; the historical NE-5B comparison used a different baseline and should not be treated as a direct paired benchmark here.
