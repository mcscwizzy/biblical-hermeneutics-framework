## Candidate Commentary

### A patterned genealogy

The chapter uses a regular genealogy pattern: the age at which a son is fathered, further years and other children, a total lifespan, and a closing notice. This pattern carries the line from Adam through Seth toward Noah. Its repeated statement, “and he died,” makes mortality a persistent refrain, while Enoch’s account interrupts that refrain. The opening also links humanity’s creation in God’s likeness with Seth being born in Adam’s likeness and image.

*Verses:* Genesis 5:1-3, Genesis 5:25-32, Genesis 5:3-20, Genesis 5:5-31<br>

### Numbers, Enoch, and Noah

The ages and lifespans in Genesis 5 are not identical in the Masoretic Text, the Septuagint, and the Samaritan Pentateuch—three textual traditions of Genesis. The differences raise a textual question about the chapter’s numbers without, on their own, settling which reading is original or allowing a single chronology to be calculated. Within the genealogy, Enoch receives an unusual notice: he walks with God and is taken by God instead of receiving the ordinary death formula. The line then reaches Noah, whose name Lamech connects with hoped-for relief from toil and from the cursed ground.

*Verses:* Genesis 5:1-32, Genesis 5:21-24, Genesis 5:25-32, Genesis 5:3-31<br>

### More than a list of dates

Read as a literary unit, the genealogy provides continuity from Adam to Noah while holding together repeated mortality and Lamech’s hope connected with Noah. This helps explain why the chapter is more than a modern chronology table: its orderly succession advances the narrative while drawing attention to the recurring deaths, Enoch’s exception, and the expectation expressed at Noah’s birth.

*Verses:* Genesis 5:1-32, Genesis 5:21-24, Genesis 5:25-32, Genesis 5:3-31<br>

### Genesis in its larger setting

Genesis moves from universal origins in chapters 1–11 to the ancestral family narratives in chapters 12–50, ending with Jacob’s household in Egypt. Genesis 5 belongs to the opening movement, where genealogy is one of the forms that carries the story forward.
